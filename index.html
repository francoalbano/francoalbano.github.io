Merrimac Energy Backup

Merrimac Energy Backup is a personal utility for collecting monthly energy-analysis packets from an existing Home Assistant installation and storing backup copies in the owner's private Google Drive.

What it does

It packages locally backed-up electricity, HVAC, weather, and data-quality records; checks archive completeness; and uses rclone to transfer the resulting files to a designated Google Drive folder for later analysis.

This is a personal-use tool. It does not offer public accounts, access to household measurements, or a hosted data-analysis service.

Google Drive access

To support automated uploads and reconciliation, the tool uses Google Drive OAuth authorization. The currently configured rclone integration requests full Google Drive scope, which permits reading, creating, updating and deleting Drive files. Its normal operations are configured to use a dedicated energy-archive folder; that folder setting is not an OAuth permission boundary.

Google Drive files remain in the owner's account and are not published by this site.

Terms of use

This tool is for the owner's personal household records and is provided without any public service or third-party access. The owner is responsible for authorizing Drive access, maintaining local backups, and reviewing stored files.
